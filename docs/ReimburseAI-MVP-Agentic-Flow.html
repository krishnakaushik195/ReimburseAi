Claim states

Human-in-the-loop (AG Grid UI)

  • Reviewers work through a queue sorted by risk score.
  • Each row shows the extracted fields, the flags, and the agent's reasons.
  • Reviewers can edit cells inline and approve or reject in bulk.
  • Every agent decision and human override is written to the audit log.

Multi-tenant hooks

  • Every record carries a tenant_id.
  • Each tenant has its own policy JSON, risk thresholds, and approval tiers.
